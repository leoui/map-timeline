How to export your Timeline on iPhone
  1. Open the Google Maps app and tap your profile photo.
  2. Go to Your Timeline.
  3. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
  4. Select Export Timeline data.
  5. Choose a date range and tap Export.
  6. Share the resulting JSON file here.

Before loading the map

Your Timeline file is never uploaded. The map provider receives the tile coordinates for map areas in the selected journey, along with normal network information such as your IP address. This may reveal places in your Timeline to the map provider.
